## How it works

1. Pick a video from the library: by topic, level (A2 to C1) or length.
2. Press **START**. The first clip plays.
3. Type what you heard and press `Enter`.
4. The app marks each word: right, almost right, missing or extra. Fix it and press `Enter` again until the green tick appears.
5. Go on to the next clip. Press `Tab` to listen again, or ask for a hint when a word will not come.

Sign in with Google and the app saves your progress, your learning time and a **daily streak**, so you can practise a little every day and pick up where you left off. You can practise without an account too.
